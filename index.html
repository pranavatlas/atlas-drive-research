About these examples +

What the arm result shows.

Recorded arm trials compare three hypothetical actuator designs with the same controller. Payload, timing and reach change which designs complete the task. Some jobs remain out of reach. These are simulated retrofits, not qualified hardware.

Where training and bench evidence fit.

The trained selector supports a specific learning benefit over fixed motion curves in simulation. The separate bench comparison supports measured calibration. Neither isolates a unique motor-model training advantage or validates the arm retrofit on hardware. Full records, including failed gates, stay internal.
